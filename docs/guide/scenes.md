# Switch sounds with scenes

A scene is another setting of the same rig: which units are on, where every knob
sits, and how loud it plays. A tone can hold up to four. Use them for the parts of
one song, such as a clean verse, a pushed chorus, and a louder solo.

Moving between scenes is instant. Nothing reloads, so there's no gap in the sound,
and delay and reverb tails ring on into the next scene.

## Add and switch scenes

The scene bar sits under the chain strip.

1. Dial in the first sound.
2. Press **Add scene**. You now have **Scene 1** and **Scene 2**, both set the
   same, and you're on Scene 2.
3. Change knobs and switch units on or off for the new part.
4. Press ++1++, ++2++, ++3++, or ++4++, or click a scene, to switch.

**Remove scene** deletes the scene you're on. When only one is left, the tone goes
back to a single sound, with the settings of that scene.

## What belongs to a scene

| Change | Applies to |
| --- | --- |
| Turning a knob | The scene you're on |
| Switching a unit on or off | The scene you're on |
| The level knob in the scene bar | The scene you're on |
| Adding, moving, or removing a unit | Every scene |
| Loading a capture, cabinet IR, or TONE3000 model | Every scene |

A new unit arrives in every scene with the settings it had when you added it.

Saving a tone saves all its scenes, and switching scenes doesn't count as an edit,
so it doesn't mark the tone as unsaved.

## Trails

Switching off a delay or reverb, by footswitch, key, or scene, stops new sound
going into it but lets what's already ringing fade out, the way a pedal with trails
does. Switch it back on and it starts fresh.

## Keep tones at the same volume

The knob at the right of the scene bar is the tone's level, or the scene's level
when the tone has scenes. It sits after every unit, so it changes how loud the tone
is without changing how hard the amp is driven. Use it for a solo boost.

**Match level** plays a strummed test chord through the tone and sets its level to
sit with the factory tones, so paging through your tones doesn't jump in volume.
Match each scene separately if you want them even, or leave the solo louder.

The **Output** knob in the header is still your overall volume for everything.
