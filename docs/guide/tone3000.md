# Find tones on TONE3000

[TONE3000](https://www.tone3000.com) is a community library of NAM captures and
impulse responses of real gear. Amp GPUI can load them straight onto the chain.

You need a free TONE3000 account. Your own captures and the factory tones work
without one.

## Sign in

1. Press **Browse tones** at the right of the chain strip.
2. Press **Continue**. TONE3000 opens in your browser.
3. Enter your email and the 6-digit code it sends you.
4. Pick a tone, or close the page. Either way, the app takes over from there.

The app keeps you signed in, encrypted to your Windows account. **Sign out** in the
panel forgets the session, and tones you already downloaded stay in your library.

## Load a tone

The panel lists your **Favorites**, **Created**, and **Downloaded** tones, plus
**Trending** and **Latest**. **Browse TONE3000** opens the full catalog.

Pick a tone to see its details, then pick a model from the pack. It downloads and
goes on the chain. Pick another model to swap it in place, so you can compare them
while you play.

To change the tone in one unit, select it and press **Browse tones** in its details.
The catalog then shows only what fits: amps, pedals, or IRs. Press **Tone details**
to get back to a loaded tone's other models.

Only NAM and IR tones can load. The app shows other formats but can't run them.

## Sharing tones that use them

Tones from TONE3000 belong to their creators, and each has its own license, shown in
its details. TONE3000's terms don't allow passing the files around, so:

- A [tone pack](sharing.md#share-one-tone-tone-packs) leaves TONE3000 files out and
  keeps a reference. Whoever imports it downloads them through their own account the
  first time they load the tone.
- A library folder you sync with other people would carry those files along, so
  keep tones from TONE3000 out of a shared library.
